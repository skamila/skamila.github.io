I am a Software Engineer with 7 years of commercial experience in software development, working mainly with Java.

I also gained experience in a large corporate environment, where my scope expanded beyond core software development to a Security Champion role. I view software development as a team game where technical knowledge meets effective interpersonal skills. I naturally look beyond just writing code-taking responsibility for the team’s overall workflow by engaging in technical onboarding, code reviews, recruitment, and developer support. My strengths lie in strong organization and reliability, making me comfortable with planning tasks and keeping processes running smoothly.

Ultimately, I am committed to high code quality, reliable end-to-end delivery, and fostering an open and supportive team environment.
